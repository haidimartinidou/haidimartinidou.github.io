    <p>Parallaxi is a long-standing independent free press magazine based in Thessaloniki, Greece, with a strong focus on culture, public life, urban identity, and contemporary social commentary. For an international audience, it can be understood as a local cultural magazine and alternative city publication, similar in spirit to an independent city paper in the United States.</p>
    <p>My articles for Parallaxi include public-facing essays at the intersection of neuroscience, technology, music, and society. In these pieces, I explored topics such as brain-machine interfaces, sensory augmentation, neuroplasticity, and the emotional dynamics of collective experiences such as live concerts. Writing for Parallaxi allowed me to translate scientific ideas into accessible language while connecting them to everyday culture, public imagination, and contemporary life.</p>
