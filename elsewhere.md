      <h2>Research Studentship, University of Glasgow</h2>
    </div>
    <p>From 3 to 7 August 2026 I visited the Anatomy Facility at the University of Glasgow as an affiliate researcher, hosted by Dr Ourania Varsou (Senior Lecturer in Anatomy, School of Medicine, Dentistry and Nursing). It was also a small return, since my BSc in Neuroscience was at Glasgow too.</p>
    <p>The core of the work is the AAUP exchange-programme study, which continues remotely since the visit. It looks at what makes academic exchange in medical education work well for participants from conflict-affected areas. Since the visit I have written the codebook, run the quantitative analysis, built the thematic analysis, drafted the literature review, Methods, Results and Discussion, and co-authored the conference abstract as joint first author, with the aim of shaping this into a transferable framework for running such exchanges successfully. During the week I also had a chance to shadow the development of imaging phantoms with research assistant Kate MacDiarmid, and spent some time at the university's Imaging Centre with Dr Michael Stringer to see MRI scanning in practice.</p>
