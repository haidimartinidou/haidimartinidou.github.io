I always wondered how we coin terms, and thought I'd try my hand at it today:

Homo techiens (n.): the generation that Googled its symptoms before it learned to drink water.

It's 2018. A lady in her 20s wakes up at 1PM.

Googles ... "Swollen under-eyes meaning".

She feels her youth should magically override the stressors of her chosen lifestyle. It's hard to combine parties and grades without taking a toll on your sleep, and yet she wonders.

The dehydrated young adult decides to have two scones for breakfast, and a cafe-ccino all-cino from a trendy spot. As she takes a sip, she reads:

"In some cases, under-eye swelling could be a sign of infection or another serious underlying condition. Medication may be necessary to help with your symptoms."

That's it.

What a misfortune, and so young.

Calls a friend to announce that she's probably suffering from a serious underlying condition.

Friend: *awkward silence*

"Sam, have you considered the possibility of drinking more than 200ml of water per day?"

Will our protagonist improve her habits or will she continue to embark on a journey of cyberchondria?

Let's fast forward to 2025. We're the generation that makes fitness cool again. Ergo, we choose wearables to gain access to a more tailored approach than a search engine. The personalisation makes the data more reliable. And the narrative changes:

A boy wakes up in his 20s after a good 9-hour sleep.

He feels very fine.

Checks his magic ring, as we are now in 2025 and that's a thing.

Magic Ring: "You must feel awful. What have you done? Did you not sleep with swan feathers as I recommended? You need 18000mg of electrolytes and a 28-hour run."

But he feels very ok?

He must be wrong. He was always underestimating his health problems. May this ring save the world like it saved his life today.

Yet again, what is our reality other than our perception of it? And what better advisor than our own natural cues?

If we were to enter an era of strong technology integration in our daily lives, we should focus on maintaining a strong relationship with our internal well-being, and the ability to check our vitals naturally. We are super-capable of finding a lovely and sustainable equilibrium where we know

- When to take a break
- When to do our best
- When to stretch out our efforts to achieve a new milestone

Let's do it all in sync with our nature. May tech be an aid, and not a new dependency.
