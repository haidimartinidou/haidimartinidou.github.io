[HAIDI: replace this whole post with your first real one.]

To publish something new, add a markdown file to the `_posts/` folder, named `YYYY-MM-DD-a-short-title.md`, with front matter like this at the top:

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "Your Title"
date: 2026-07-10
excerpt: "One or two sentences shown on the Odd Things index and homepage."
---

Your writing goes here, in normal markdown.
```

As soon as that file exists, Jekyll picks it up automatically:

- it gets its own page
- it appears on the [Odd Things](/articles/) index
- the most recent entries also show up on the homepage

No other steps are needed. You don't have to edit any index page or navigation file by hand.
