Mixed-methods evaluation with Dr Ourania Varsou (joint first authors) of a medical exchange programme for students from conflict-affected settings. I built the master dataset and codebook, ran the quantitative analysis, and built the reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke), integrating both into a transferable framework for international medical exchange programmes.

Abstract submitted to the 20th Annual University of Glasgow Learning &amp; Teaching Conference (April 2027); manuscript in preparation.
